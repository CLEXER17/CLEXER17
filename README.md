## About Me

I'm **Clexer**, a full-stack developer and automation builder based in **Odisha, India**, working at the intersection of trading systems, AI infrastructure, and web engineering. I design and ship bots that automate trading and copy-trading workflows, host and serve AI models, and build web applications end to end — from backend architecture to frontend delivery.

I approach engineering with a product mindset: reliability, security, and measurable impact come before flash. Outside of code, I'm drawn to how medicine and technology intersect — the history of medical breakthroughs listed below is a constant source of inspiration for the kind of high-impact engineering I want to build toward.

**Open To:** freelance & contract engineering work, collaboration on trading/automation tooling, and AI infrastructure projects.
